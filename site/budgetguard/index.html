Why this gate exists

  • Coverage gate: the evaluated window must be covered by real source samples (≤10% missing, no gap > 20 s per slot), computed with count_over_time over 15 s buckets from two required series per slot — sparse telemetry cannot pass.
  • Fractional counts are preserved; count-like values are rounded only where the contract says so.
  • Fixtures are validated (presence, finiteness, relationships) before replay, so a malformed fixture is rejected instead of replayed into a green result.
  • history_complete is false for every lab window: a 5-minute canary never proves a 30-day SLO.
